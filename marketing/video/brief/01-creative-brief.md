# Creative brief: "The money lives in the chat"

Owner: the director. Version 2 (2026-10-22): timings and finish updated from brief/research-hypermotion.md; 03-storyboard.md v2 has the binding film-wide grammar.

## The one idea
**The group chat already decides everything on a trip, except the money. Pot puts the money in the chat, with rules.**

The brand mark is a cooking pot that is also a chat bubble with typing dots. The video is built on that object.

## The thread: three dots
The typing dots carry the story from start to end.
1. **Act 1 (anxiety):** "Leo is typing…" never turns into a payment. The chat's dots mean waiting and chasing.
2. **Act 2 (transformation):** we push into the dots. They become the logo's dots, and the pot-bubble forms around them on a marigold flood.
3. **Act 3 (the rules at work):** the same dots pulse as "Checking Pot rules…", then "Approved by Pot rules" lands with the marigold burst.
4. **Act 5 (rest):** the dots sit still inside the lockup.

## Tone
- Fast, warm, confident and a little funny (the "Leo is typing…" joke).
- Premium fintech polish, never crypto-bro. No neon, no glitch-everywhere, no blockchain cubes.
- The product looks friendly. The tech section looks precise.

## Structure (≈ 90 s, 60 fps, 120 BPM grid)

| Act | Palette | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The problem | Night (`#1c1424`), grain 2-3 %, vignette ≤ 10 % | 0:00-0:16 | The hook, the friend who fronts the money, the chase, and why split apps and shared accounts fail |
| 2. The reveal | Marigold flood → cream | 0:16-0:22 | Dots → logo → "Group chat money that follows its own rules." |
| 3. How it works | Day (cream `#faf9f6`) | 0:22-1:10 | Create with rules → share → join in seconds → instant spend → group approval → close with fair refunds |
| 4. Under the hood | Night, x-ray | 1:10-1:23 | Every Pot is a Sputnik DAO on NEAR; the rules contract; gasless; no one can take money out alone |
| 5. End card | Cream | 1:23-1:30 | Lockup, tagline, "Built at NEAR HACKATHON", Social Money · Real-World Money |

## Visual language
- **Type is the hero of the problem act.** Huge Gabarito 800 words fill the frame and are cropped by the edges. Key words sit on a member-colour highlight block with eggplant text (no marigold before the reveal).
- **The phone is the hero of the product act.**
  - A 3D-tilted phone (±12-22°) on cream, with the camera always alive: subtle noise drift and expo-in-out punch-ins.
  - Key UI cards **break out of the phone** toward the camera: the "Approved by Pot rules" card, the approval meter, the refund table.
  - The rule sentences appear as big kinetic type beside the phone, in the app's own words.
- **The people are colours.** Maya is `member-1` yellow, Sam is `member-2` mint and Leo is `member-3` periwinkle. Avatars become characters: they pop in, fly between phones and carry money chips.
- **Stickers (AI images)** add real-world flavour in small doses: a Lisbon tram, a grocery bag, a boat, a receipt, a pastel de nata. They are die-cut, glossy, in one consistent style, and pop in with overshoot beside the phone.
- **Depth and finish:**
  - Token shadows on flat cream (no member-colour glows).
  - Grain at very low opacity in Night acts only.
  - Fake directional motion blur on whips.
  - No lens flares, no rainbow light leaks.
- **Transitions are motivated, never random:**
  - A whip on a camera move.
  - A zoom-through into the next thing we talk about.
  - A circle iris from a tapped button.
  - The marigold flood out of the typing dots.
  - The logo tile morphing into the phone screen (Act 2 → 3), since the tile and the phone screen share the rounded-square shape.
  - A camera whip between Maya's and Leo's phones in one shared space, anchored by a flying card (no 3D flips).
  - The phone turning into an x-ray for Act 4.

## Sound
- The music has one 120 BPM track in sections:
  - Problem: tense, ticking, muted plucks and a sub pulse, building.
  - A riser that ends in a big warm hit on the logo.
  - Bright, bouncy groove for the product.
  - Filtered and deeper for the tech section.
  - A resolving final chord on the end card.
- The voice-over is a friendly, energetic narrator. Duck the music by 8-10 dB under the voice.
- SFX follow a sound system, not a sound per motion: 3-5 kinds and 1-3 hand-picked hits per act. The dots have their own `blips` motif, "Approved by Pot rules" gets `success` + `burst`, and the reveal gets the `impact`.
- Loudness target: about −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.

## Must land (the checklist a judge should leave with)
1. The problem is real and specific: fronting, chasing, and split apps that hold no money.
2. Pot holds the group's money in dollars, and its rules are plain sentences.
3. Joining takes seconds: a link and a Google login, with no wallet, seed phrase or gas.
4. Small spends approve themselves under the rules, instantly.
5. Bigger spends go to the group (2 of 3).
6. Closing gives everyone their fair share back ($22.00 each).
7. Under the hood:
   - Each Pot is a Sputnik DAO on NEAR.
   - A rules contract (the Pot Hub) auto-approves only what the rules allow.
   - Every action is gasless (meta-transactions).
   - No one can take money out alone.

## Guardrails
- Never claim features we don't ship: no cross-chain deposits, no mainnet, no cash-out.
- The D-61 numbers are exact.
- The app UI copy is real.
- No full account IDs, no keys, no hashes on screen.
