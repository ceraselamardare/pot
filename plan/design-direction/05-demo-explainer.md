# Design direction 05: the demo explainer (`/demo`)

Written 2026-10-27 for Cerasela (P2, design lead) and the `/demo` builder. It replaces today's `/demo` (persona forms + snapshot) with a guided, simulated walkthrough and moves the live persona demo to `/demo/live`.

1. **One path, six chapters, one Next button.** The visitor walks the D-61 story (Lisbon Trip: Maya, Sam, Leo) from "start a Pot" to "$22 back each". Every chapter is a short list of beats. One [Next] button steps through every beat of every chapter, so nobody has to choose where to look.
2. **Two panes that never disagree.** "The app" shows the real Pot UI, built from the real components with simulated data. "Under the hood" shows what that tap really means: what was signed, what our relayer sent, the receipts, the Sputnik proposal and its votes, and who paid. Both panes are driven by the same beat, so they change together.
3. **Plain words first, details on demand.** Every beat has one plain sentence. Below it sits a decoded card that names each field and says in a few words what it means. The raw JSON sits behind a disclosure. A curious friend reads the first level, a judge the second, an engineer the third.
4. **Nothing is real, and nothing pretends to be.** No wallet, no RPC, no relayer call. Every hash starts with `sim-`, every simulated id carries a "Simulated" chip, and every chapter links to a real equivalent on testnet, styled differently. The simulation is generated by the same engine our tests trust (`@pot/sdk/testing` MockChain), and its receipt chains are copied from real testnet runs. So it is made up, but exact.
5. **The brand's calm, with one celebration.** The page uses the app's tokens, motion and copy rules. The Night-scoped "Under the hood" panel is the landing's x-ray act, now made the size of a page. The 600 ms marigold burst plays once, in chapter 4, when the visitor taps [Get $84 back] and Pot rules approve it.

## 1. Goals and readers

| Reader | Arrives from | What they must leave with | How the page serves them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A curious human** (a friend, a hackathon visitor; Leo, in short) | the landing's [Try the demo], a link in a chat | "A Pot is the group's account. Small spends approve themselves, big ones need the group, and everyone gets their share back. I never paid a fee." | The app pane and the plain beat captions alone tell the whole story. They never have to open a card. |
| **A human judge** (NEAR-literate, 3-5 minutes) | README J-1, the submission page | "Every Pot is a real Sputnik DAO. The Hub holds a 1-of-1 rulebot seat. Users sign NEP-366 delegates and the relayer pays. Close is one atomic proposal. And it's honest about what is simulated." | The decoded cards in the hood (signer, receiver, method, args, gas, deposit, nonce), the receipt route, the proposal card with its threshold math, the "who paid" strip, and one real tx per chapter. |
| **An AI judge** reading server HTML | a fetch of `/demo`, `/demo/1`…`/demo/6`, or `/demo/all` | The same facts as the human judge, as text | Every chapter is server-rendered in its final state: all beats in order, every card, the raw JSON inside `<details>` (still text in the HTML), real hashes as links, a glossary `<dl>`. No canvas, no text in images, no content that needs JS. |

**Success criteria** (testable):
- The whole path takes ≤ 4 min at one Next per 5 s (about 45 beats), and ≤ 90 s reading only the app pane and captions.
- With JS off, `/demo/4` contains the strings "Approved by Pot rules", "request_spend", "VoteApprove", "84000000", "Simulated" and a link to `https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/…`.
- The page makes zero network requests to an RPC, `/api/relay` or `/api/demo/*` (Playwright asserts it).
- Exactly one marigold control is visible in any state (M-20).

**Non-goals:** no live chain reads, no account creation, and no real signing in the browser. Signing and the live persona demo stay at `/demo/live` (§3.6). The demo covers only the D-61 happy path: no failures, refunds or rule changes. Those get one line each in the glossary ("What if a payout fails?").

They can switch back any time; the other tab then shows a small `ink` dot plus the visually hidden "new" when it has changed since they last looked (not a status colour, since "new" isn't a state).
- **The phone on phones:** the app pane shows the phone at `min(100%, 20rem)` wide and aspect 390/720 (a slightly shorter crop), centred, with no bezel below 360 px (`PhoneFrame` `className` + a `bezel={false}` prop, §8).
- **Beat bar:** 44 px icon buttons ← → with visible labels for screen readers ("Back", "Next"); the caption gets two lines (`line-clamp-2`), and a tap on it scrolls to the full caption in the hood.
- **Chapter switcher:** a `<details>` (works without JS) with the six chapter links, 56 px rows.
- **320:** raw JSON `<pre>` blocks scroll horizontally inside themselves (`overflow-x: auto`, `tabindex="0"`, `role="region"` with an `aria-label`); everything else wraps. Ids break with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` inside `dd`s.

### 3.5 Navigation and keyboard

| Input | Effect |
|---|---|
| [Next] / [Back] | one beat forward or back; Next past the last beat goes to the next chapter (a normal link navigation, so history works) |
| the in-phone primary ([Create Pot], [Get $84 back], [Approve $300]…) | the same as Next on its beat. On any other beat the phone shows no actionable primary |
| `→` / `←` | Next / Back, when focus is not in a text field, a `<pre>` region, a `<summary>` or the tablist (where arrows move between tabs). Advertised with `aria-keyshortcuts="ArrowRight"` / `"ArrowLeft"` |
| `Escape` | closes an open glossary popover |
| a chapter rail link | that chapter at beat 1 |
| a beat title in the hood timeline (past beats only) | jumps back to that beat |
| browser back/forward | chapters are pages; beats update the URL fragment with `history.replaceState` (no history entry per beat) |

- **Focus:** after Next or Back, focus stays on the button pressed. A single polite live region (`aria-live="polite"`, in the beat bar) speaks "Step 5 of 9. <caption>". Jumps from the rail land focus on the chapter H1.
- **Progress:** "Step n of N" in the beat bar, the rail's done/current marks, and `<progress>`-like semantics via `aria-valuenow` on the bar (`role="group" aria-label="Chapter progress"`). The visitor's furthest beat per chapter is remembered in `localStorage` (`pot-demo-progress`), so the rail can show Done ticks. Rendering never depends on it.

### 3.6 No-JS and AI-judge rendering

- **Server HTML is the chapter's final state**, written for reading top to bottom:
  1. the simulation banner;
  2. the H1 + aha;
  3. **the app pane** with the phone at its *final* screen (e.g. chapter 4: "$84 sent to you", $366.00), followed by an `<ol>` "What happened in the app" with each app beat's caption;
  4. **the hood** with every beat card rendered (not collapsed), raw JSON inside **closed** `<details>` (still text in the HTML);
  5. After, Real ones (links), Glossary `<dl>`;
  6. prev/next chapter links.
- **No tabs without JS:** the tablist is rendered only after hydration; until then both panes stack (app, then hood).
- **Nothing is hidden by `opacity: 0` in the server render** (03, 04 rule 6). JS arms the "not yet reached" state only after hydration, and only below or outside the viewport.
- **Theme:** the tokens' `prefers-color-scheme` fallback applies (brand §7); the hood's `data-theme="night"` is in the server HTML.
- **Persona forms:** `/demo/live` keeps the real POST forms, which work without JS (unchanged).
- **Machine-readable bonus (Could):** `/demo/script.json` serves the committed dataset (§7) with `Content-Type: application/json`, linked from the end card as "The whole simulation as JSON".

### 4.3 The receipt route (the flow diagram)

**Lane layout (hood ≥ 560 px):** a sequence diagram.

  - Icons: device `smartphone`, relayer `send`, person = their `Avatar` (member colour), Hub `list-checks`, Pot `users` (the group's account; never a vault, coin or bank icon), USDC `banknote`.
  - The **Pot Hub** node gets the landing's 4 px `brand` top edge, the only marigold in the hood.
- **Lifelines:** 1 px `line` from each node down the card.
- **Each receipt is a row** (min 48 px): a 2 px `line-strong` arrow from the caller's lane to the callee's lane, with the method in 600 weight above it and its chips below (§4.3.1). Rows are numbered 1…n in the left gutter (`body-sm`, tabular).
  - **Then-callbacks** (`.then`, e.g. `on_spend_checked`): a dashed arrow; on the same lane it's a 16 px loop on the lifeline, labelled "then: on_spend_checked".
  - **Parallel calls** (`Promise::and`, chapter 4 rows 3-5): a bracket in the gutter labelled "in parallel".
  - **Batch receipts** (several actions in one receipt: `CreateAccount + Transfer + UseGlobalContract + new`; `Transfer prepay + add_proposal`; the three close `ft_transfer`s): one arrow with a stacked list of actions, labelled "one receipt, N actions".

**List layout (hood < 560 px):** each `<li>` is a stacked row:
`3  Pot Hub → USDC` (label) / `ft_balance_of(Lisbon Trip)` / chips. The caller and callee show their icon or avatar. Then-callbacks are indented 16 px with a 2 px `line` rule on the left; parallel calls share a bracket label.

### 5.2 The one burst
- **Where:** chapter 4, beat 7 (Pot rules approve the $84), from the in-phone [Get $84 back] button's position, with the result card's `brand-soft` tint and the check pop (02 §4, ux-spec §6.3).
- **When:** only when beat 7 is reached by the visitor's own forward action (the phone button or Next/→ from beat 6), and **once per page view**.

- **The fix from 04 applies:** opacity held to 40 %, so the particles read for about 350 ms.

### 5.3 Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`)

### 5.4 Don't
Scroll-driven sequences (the demo is step-driven, not scroll-driven), looping traces, a moving "packet" dot along the arrows (04 explains why it blips), 3D phone tilt, typing dots on the Hub, confetti for close, or animating the focus ring.

| role / seat | A named group in the DAO with its own powers. A Pot has two: `member` (the friends) and `rulebot` (only the Pot Hub). |
| threshold | How many approvals a role needs. Members need a majority: 2 of 3 here. |
| quorum | The minimum number of approvals ever accepted. Pot sets it to 1, so nothing can pass with zero votes. |
| rulebot | The Pot Hub's seat in every Pot. One vote from it approves transfers and new members, and the Hub only casts it when the Pot's rules allow. |
| Pot Hub | Pot's own contract (`pots.potprev.testnet`). It creates each Pot, checks the rules, holds the rulebot seat and keeps the ledger of who put in what. |
| policy | The DAO's rulebook: its roles, their powers, thresholds and the 7-day voting period. |
| delegate action | A request the user signs but doesn't send (NEP-366). Someone else wraps it in a transaction and pays for it; contracts still see the user as the caller. |
| relayer | Pot's server account (`users.potprev.testnet`) that sends signed requests and pays their gas, so users need 0 NEAR. It refuses anything outside an allowlist. |
| transaction | What gets submitted to NEAR. Here the relayer signs it, and the user's request rides inside. |
| receipt | One step of a transaction's work, run by one account. A transaction fans out into receipts as contracts call each other. |
| callback (then) | A receipt that runs after another finishes and reads its result. |
| gas / Tgas | The work a call may use, bought in NEAR. 1 Tgas is a trillion gas, about 0.0001 NEAR. Unused gas is refunded. |
| yoctoNEAR | The smallest unit of NEAR: 10⁻²⁴ NEAR. Token transfers must attach exactly 1, to prove a full-access key signed them. |
| deposit | NEAR attached to a call. In Pot it is 0 everywhere except that 1 yoctoNEAR. |
| storage prepay | NEAR the Hub sends with each proposal, so the Pot can pay for the bytes it stores. |
| nonce | A counter on each key. Every signature uses a higher one, so an old signature can't be replayed. |
| max_block_height | The block after which a signed request expires: about 2 minutes here. |
| device key | The key Pot keeps on your phone (non-extractable). It signs your requests without a popup. |
| ed25519 signature | The kind of digital signature NEAR uses: 64 bytes that prove a key signed exactly this message. |
| invite key | A key made for one invite link. Its secret lives after the # in the link; the Hub stores only its public half. |
| account (named) | A NEAR account like `u-3p8w2kdn.users.potprev.testnet`. Pot creates it for you with 0 NEAR; your name lives off-chain. |
| USDC (test) | Pot's test dollar token on testnet (NEP-141, 6 decimals). It has no value. |
| `ft_transfer_call` | Send tokens to a contract and call it in the same step. If the contract refuses, the tokens come back. |
| global contract | Contract code stored once on NEAR and used by many accounts by its hash. Every Pot uses Sputnik's this way, for 0.012 NEAR instead of 5.4. |
| sync | A Hub method anyone can call to copy a proposal's final status from the Pot into the Hub's records. |
| pro-rata | Split by what each person put in: everyone put in $150, so everyone gets an equal third. |

## 7. The deterministic dataset

### 7.1 Principle
**Generated once from MockChain, committed, then imported.** No browser computation, no randomness, no clock. The page imports `apps/web/app/demo/_data/demo-script.json` and renders it. A test regenerates it and asserts byte equality, so it can't drift from the engine.

### 7.2 How to generate it (`scripts/gen-demo-script.ts`, run with `scripts/run gen-demo-script`)
1. `const cfg = testnetConfig("potprev")`; `new MockChain({cfg, startMs: Date.UTC(2026, 10, 7, 9, 0), startHeight: 275_000_000, msPerBlock: 600})`.
2. **Keys:** for each person, an ed25519 keypair from `seed = sha256("pot-demo/v1/" + name)` (in memory only; **never written** to the JSON or any file, open question 8). `chain.createAccount(account, publicKey)`. The invite seed is `sha256("pot-demo/v1/invite")`.
3. **Script:** the steps of `fixtures.ts`'s `build()`, in the same order and with the same time gaps, but with the §2.0 accounts, the slug `lisbon-trip-7k2f` and `KeyPairPotSigner` instead of `relayIntent`, so every delegate is **really signed and verified** by `MockChain.handleRelayBody`. Capture `txLog`, `eventLog` and, before and after each step, `hub.getPot`, `hub.getMembers`, `dao.getPolicy`, `dao.getProposals`, `usdc.balanceOf` for every account.
4. **Hashes:** each MockChain tx hash is replaced by `simHash(txId) = "sim-" + base58(sha256("pot-demo/v1/tx/" + txId)).slice(0, 32)`, where `txId` is e.g. `c4.request`. Block hashes use `"pot-demo/v1/block/" + height`, also with the `sim-` prefix.
5. **Receipts:** MockChain doesn't model receipts or gas, so each `SimTx.receipts` comes from a **receipt template** per method (`_data/receipt-templates.ts`), filled with the step's ids, amounts, proposal ids, events and return values from MockChain. The templates mirror the real chains of §2 (attached gas, deposits, memos, order). Burnt gas and NEAR are copied per template from the real runs and marked `approx: true`.
6. **Prepay:** computed with a TS twin of the hub's `prepay_bytes` + `bytes_cost` (`contracts/pot-hub/src/lib.rs` §3.11 model: `PREPAY_PROPOSAL_OVERHEAD_BYTES 250`, `FIRST_VOTE 160`, `EXTRA_VOTE 100`, plus the description and kind lengths, at 10¹⁹ yocto per byte).
7. **Copy** is not in the JSON: beats reference copy keys (`demo.c4.b7`). The JSON is data only.

### 7.4 Invariants (asserted by tests)
- Balances at chapter ends equal `LISBON_EXPECTED`: $300 (interlude), $450, $366, $66, $0; every person's NEAR is 0 before and after every step.
- Proposal ids 0-4, request ids 0-1, refs and descriptions exactly as §2.
- Every `hash` matches `^sim-[1-9A-HJ-NP-Za-km-z]{32}$`; every `real.hash` matches `^[1-9A-HJ-NP-Za-km-z]{43,44}$`, and the two sets are disjoint.
- Every `delegate` verifies (`verifySignedDelegate`) and decodes back from `transport_base64` byte-identically (`decodeSignedDelegate`).
- Each receipt template's method sequence equals the real chain recorded in `_data/real-chains.json`, a committed, read-only snapshot of the §2 real transactions (methods, attached gas, deposits). **Should:** a network-gated script `scripts/run check-demo-real` re-fetches them (archival FastNEAR, read-only, `with-testnet-lock.sh` not needed since it only reads) and diffs.
- The JSON contains no `private_key`, `secret_key` or 64-byte seed (gitleaks + a grep test).
